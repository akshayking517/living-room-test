# Living Room Test

A one-page check: can a very smart computer sit in the living room like a silent Wi-Fi box, or does it get too hot to touch?

Open `index.html` in a browser. No install.

- **Brain size** — everyday assistant, expert specialist, or superhuman brain
- **Chip type** — traditional compute or compute-in-memory
- **Room weather** — air-conditioned, normal, or a hot summer day
- **Run 1,000 days of use** — another stretch of ordinary days, including the hottest the box got
